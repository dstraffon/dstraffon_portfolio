Playground

Small interactive components I built for fun. These are random ideas that I felt the need to bring to life.

Press play to try one here, or open any of them in their own tab.

Croc Swamp

An illustrated swamp you can mess with. Tap a croc to give it orders, draw a circle to hatch a new one, or tap the clock to skip to night.

Open in a new tab ↗

Spell Speaker²

A made-up synth with a live oscilloscope. Turn the knobs and cast harmonics to reshape the wave, then switch it on to hear it.

Open in a new tab ↗

How a record plays

Two interactive diagrams of a turntable and its cartridge, showing how a stylus following one spiral groove turns into an electrical signal.

Open in a new tab ↗

Slot machine reel bank

A cut-open slot machine. Pull the handle and a random number for each reel decides where its motor stops it.
