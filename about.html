Who am I?

I’m a UX and product designer with 8+ years of experience across enterprise software, marketplaces and consulting. As lead designer at Asset Layer I worked on SaaS, marketplace, and game design that drove $400,000+ in revenue, redesigned onboarding to cut new user churn by 35%, and built a cross platform design system.

I’ve run 30+ user testing sessions across freelance and in house work, and I have a technical background in QA, front end collaboration and data driven research. I care about products that are accessible and tested with real users.

Having worked for clients in both the private and public sector, I try to blend the analog and the digital to steer focus towards human centered designs.

Outside of design I love cats and dogs, emerging technology and being outside. I’m fascinated by music and by making my own. I love talking about sound design and synthesizers, and I taught myself guitar (poorly) and piano (also poorly).
